We do have some terms and conditions for delivery though!

  • Delivery is only available on weekdays (excluding Malaysia Public Holidays).

Orders & Payment

Browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to create an account or log in before completing your purchase.

We accept major credit/debit cards, online banking, and select e-wallets. All available options will be shown at checkout.

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. Please contact our support team as soon as possible if you need to make changes.

Product Delivery

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the city, and 5–7 business days for other regions. Express delivery options are available at checkout.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can also view your order status anytime under your account dashboard.

Currently we only deliver within the country. International shipping is something we're working on for the future!

Account & Returns

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened or used items are not eligible for return due to hygiene reasons.
